## About this entry

This entry is a single travel-site style review written in the voice of a
well-travelled alien who has clearly reviewed other planets before. It opens
with a plain score, slips in a genuine compliment, and then complains about
two things a visitor from elsewhere would honestly find wasteful: a planet
mostly covered in ocean that nobody actually drinks, and hosts who disappear
into sleep for a third of the stay. The closing line keeps the reviewer's
tone — willing to return, but on better-rested terms — so the joke lands as
an outsider misreading ordinary Earth life rather than as a spaceman gag.
